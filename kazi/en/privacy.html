This policy explains what personal data the Swala Kazi app collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and your rights under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (PDPA).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is [LEGAL ENTITY], [ADDRESS], Tanzania. Registration with the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC): [PDPC REGISTRATION NUMBER]. For anything about your data, email support@kemosabeonline.uk.

2. Our rules for your data

  • We collect only what a booking, a payment or safety needs.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • The other side of a booking never sees your phone number. Calls and chat stay inside the app.
  • Your exact address is shared with a helper only for a confirmed booking.

4. Location

The app uses your phone's location only when you use a feature that needs it: choosing an address, a helper tapping "On my way" or "I've arrived", and SOS. Helpers share live location only from "On my way" until "I've arrived". We don't track anyone in the background outside a job.

5. Why we are allowed to use your data

  • To perform our agreement with you: accounts, bookings, payments, chat and calls.
  • Legal obligations: tax records and valid requests from the police or courts.
  • Legitimate interests: safety, preventing fraud and duplicate accounts, keeping evidence for disputes.
  • Your consent: for ID photos, the video selfie and location. You can withdraw consent, but we can't then verify you or run a job that needs it.

6. Who we share data with

  • The other side of a booking: only what the job needs, as in the table above.
  • Our licensed payment partner: to collect payments, pay helpers and send refunds.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: our server host, Cloudflare (network security), Google Firebase (notifications) and our email provider. They act only on our instructions.
  • The police or a court: when they make a valid legal request, for example after a theft or safety report. We share a helper's checked identity with the police on such a request.

Our servers are in the European Union, so your data is stored outside Tanzania. We transfer it only to providers that protect it to the standard the PDPA requires.

7. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). ID documents are stored privately and opened only by the verification team through short-lived links. Access to the system is limited to the people who need it, and admin tools are behind an extra login.

8. Your rights under the PDPA, 2022

  • See the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete your data. Some records are kept for the time shown above because the law or dispute evidence requires it.
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, a use of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

Email support@kemosabeonline.uk from the email address on your account, or say which phone number the account uses. We may ask you to confirm it is you, and we reply within 30 days. To delete your account, see Delete your account.

9. Children

KAZI is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. Helpers must be 18 or older, and we check their age.

10. Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, we publish a new version here and the app asks you to accept it.
